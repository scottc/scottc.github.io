How the Matchmaking System Works

Player Role Preferences

Real-world inspired (WoW / LoL / Dota): most players prefer dealing damage.

DPS ~68%
Heal ~20%
Tank ~12%

Classes still constrain roles. Premades get a light tank/healer core then fill with bias.

Scarce Resources (Tanks & Healers)

  • Tanks are the rarest and most valuable resource.
  • Early (low tolerance): system is conservative — prefers ideal 2 tanks / 2 healers and will not “waste” them on marginal groups if better options exist.
  • DPS are spent freely according to availability.
  • As wait grows, mins relax toward the configured floor (can be 0).
  • At full YOLO a party can form with 0 tanks and/or 0 healers so nobody is left waiting forever.

Patience & Retention (~95%)

  • Every player has a personal patience (seconds).
  • Distribution is set so that ~95% will still be waiting at the default YOLO of 300 s.
  • If wait exceeds patience the player abandons the queue (tracked in the stats bar).
  • Goal of the YOLO curve: zero players left waiting past the frustration threshold while still making a good-faith attempt at quality first.

Tower Floors (101 total)

  • Each player has an estimated average / median floor reached.
  • Higher floors = harder; floor 101 has the best loot.
  • Design pressure: parties often kick “dead weight” around floor 100 to maximise clear chance / loot.
  • Shown on player cards for future behaviour simulation.

Auto Population & Regions

  • Each region has a live population (roughly 250–6000, centred ~3000).
  • ~1–2 % of a region’s population is “active” (queueing or in match) at any time.
  • Auto mode injects new solo / small premade traffic proportional to region size.
  • Cross-play (after configurable delay): OCE↔SEA and NA↔SA become eligible (simulates extra loading / technical friction). Other region pairs stay strict.

Time-based Relaxation

  • 0 → Ideal Wait: tight ELO/level, ideal roles, preferred size, same region only.
  • Ideal → YOLO: linear widening of gaps, role mins drop (can reach 0), size can shrink, cross-play opens.
  • At YOLO: form any legal party that meets absolute mins so the queue drains.
  • Oldest players/groups are always preferred as seeds. Premades stay together.
